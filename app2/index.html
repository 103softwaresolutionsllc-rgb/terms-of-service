Avery's Riddle Me This? is designed for players of all ages — kids and adults alike. If a player is under 13, a parent or guardian should review these pages and set up the account.

🎮 Getting Started

  • Create an account or sign in from the login screen
  • Choose a difficulty on the home screen: Easy, Medium, Hard, or Master
  • Tap Enter The Arena to start, or try the Daily Challenge for bonus Soul Fragments

⚔️ The Riddle Arena

  • Read the riddle and type your answer in the input field
  • You have 60 seconds and up to 3 guesses per riddle
  • Wrong guesses clear the input automatically so you can retry quickly
  • Correct answers earn Soul Fragments (+2 normal, +5 daily) and grow your streak
  • Running out of time or guesses ends the round and resets your streak

💡 Hints & Soul Fragments

  • Tap Seek Insight during a riddle to reveal a hint (costs 2 Soul Fragments)
  • You start with 10 fragments. Earn more by solving riddles or buying packs
  • Open the + button in The Arena to quick-buy fragment packs mid-game

🔥 Streaks & Master Mode

  • Build a streak by solving riddles without failing a round
  • Reach a 10-streak to unlock Master difficulty for free
  • Or purchase Unlock Master Mode from the shop to skip the requirement

🔮 AI Summon

  • After a correct answer, tap Summon AI for a unique AI-generated riddle
  • The AI Summon Pass gives 50 summons without fragment cost
