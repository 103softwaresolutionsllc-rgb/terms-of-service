📚 How to Use Sports BetTracker

Quick Start Guide

🏆 Team Game Prediction

Navigate to the Teams tab to predict game outcomes between two teams.

  • Select your sport (Football, Basketball, Baseball, Hockey, MMA)
  • Fill in Team 1 and Team 2 details (name, stats, recent record, home/away)
  • Click "Predict Winner" to see AI-powered predictions
  • View winner probability, score predictions, and analysis factors
  • Use "Clear All" to reset and start a new prediction

🎯 Player Prop Bets

Navigate to the Players tab to analyze individual player prop bets.

  • Select your sport
  • Enter player name and recent stats
  • Choose prop type (Points, Rebounds, Touchdowns, etc.)
  • Set the over/under line and bet side
  • Add opponent defense rank and injury status
  • Click "Analyze Prop" for confidence predictions
  • Use "Clear All" to reset for a new analysis

🔗 Same-Game Parlay

Navigate to the Parlay tab to build multi-leg same-game parlays.

  • Add parlay legs (minimum 2 required)
  • For each leg: enter player name, prop description, and American odds
  • Click "Add Another Leg" to include more props
  • Enter your total stake amount
  • Click "Calculate Parlay" to see potential profit and total return
  • Use "Clear All" to start a new parlay

📊 Dashboard & Tracking

Track your betting performance and manage your bankroll in the Dashboard tab.

  • View total bets, wins, losses, and win rate
  • Monitor your current bankroll balance
  • Review recent betting activity

👥 Community Features

Connect with expert bettors in the Community tab.

  • Follow top bettors to see their picks
  • Copy successful bets with one tap
  • View detailed bettor profiles and win rates
  • Filter feed by Trending, Top Bettors, or Following

Enjoy smarter betting with AI-powered insights! 🎯
