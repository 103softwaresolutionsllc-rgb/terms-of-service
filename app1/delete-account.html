You can delete your Sports BetTracker account and all associated data at any time, in one of two ways.

🗑️ Delete Your Account

1. In the app

Open Sports BetTracker → go to your Profile → tap “Delete Account” → confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data stored on your device.

2. By email

Send a deletion request to 103softwaresolutionsllc@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with “Delete my Sports BetTracker account” in the subject line. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Your data

Deleting your account removes data stored on your device. Questions sent to the backend for AI processing may also leave server-side records; you can request removal of those records by emailing 103softwaresolutionsllc@gmail.com.
