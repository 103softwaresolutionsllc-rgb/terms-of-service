Welcome to Certivius

Certivius is a comprehensive learning and exam preparation platform designed for professionals seeking credentials in technology, project management, and business. With integrated study schedules, customized quizzes, and real-time AI mentoring, Certivius keeps you on track to pass your exam on the first try.

🔑 Key Platform Features

📈 Custom Learning Paths

Search or build curriculum paths tailored to specific certification exams (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Salesforce, or PMP). Certivius breaks down exam syllabi into bite-sized learning modules.

🤖 AI-Powered Coaching

Stuck on a difficult technical concept? Our AI Coach is available 24/7 to explain complex topics, test your understanding with rapid-fire questions, and provide immediate explanations when you get a practice question wrong.

📇 Smart Flashcards

Generate study decks automatically from your learning materials using AI, or construct your own custom decks. Spaced repetition algorithms help commit critical definitions, commands, and schemas to memory.

📝 Full-Length Practice Exams

Simulate the real test environment with realistic questions, timers, and scoring criteria. Review your results with comprehensive diagnostic breakdowns pinpointing your weak areas.

📊 Progress Dashboard

Track daily study streaks, record practice scores, and monitor your overall "Exam Readiness Score"—an analytical projection that estimates your chances of passing based on mock exam performance.
